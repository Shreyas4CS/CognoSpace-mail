🧠 How does a Decision Tree work?

A Decision Tree asks YES/NO questions one at a time — like a game of 20 Questions! 🎮
At each step it checks one feature (like: "Is the sender unknown?"). Based on the answer, it goes left or right until it decides: SPAM 🚫 or INBOX 📥.
